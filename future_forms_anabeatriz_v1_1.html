Como funciona

PRESENT CONTINUOUS = plano já combinado/agendado (tem data, hora, outra pessoa, ingresso…)
I'm meeting Sarah for lunch on Friday.

BE GOING TO = intenção, algo que você já decidiu antes · previsão com evidência (você está vendo)
I'm going to take a pottery class next year. · Look at those clouds — it's going to rain.

WILL = decisão na hora · oferta · promessa · previsão de opinião (I think, probably, I'm sure)
I'll get it! · I'll help you. · I won't tell anyone. · I think it'll be fine.

💡 Bônus: horários fixos (aula, voo, filme) usam present simple: The class starts at 9.

Exercício 1 — Practice

Parte 1 — Complete as frases

Complete com present continuous, be going to ou will + o verbo entre parênteses. Pense no porquê de cada escolha.

  1. A: The phone's ringing!  B: Don't worry, I (get) it.
  2. I (meet) a new client at the studio at 3 tomorrow. We booked it last week.
  3. Look at that sky! It (rain) any minute.
  4. I've made up my mind: I (go back) to work in January.
  5. I think AI (change) the way people learn languages.
  6. A: I can't open this jar.  B: Give it to me, I (do) it.
  7. We (fly) to Miami on Friday. The tickets are already booked!
  8. She's not paying attention to her form at all. She (hurt) her back.
  9. I promise I (not / tell) anyone about your surprise party.
  10. Guess what? My sister (have) a baby in March! 🍼
1. 'll get (decisão na hora)  |  2. 'm meeting (agendado)  |  3. 's going to rain (evidência)  |  4. 'm going to go back (decisão já tomada; I'm going back também funciona se já está tudo combinado)  |  5. will change (opinião)  |  6. 'll do (oferta)  |  7. 're flying (agendado)  |  8. 's going to hurt (evidência)  |  9. won't tell (promessa)  |  10. 's having / 's going to have (as duas são naturais)

Parte 2 — Escolha a opção correta

1. "Are you free tonight?" — "No, sorry. I ______ dinner with my in-laws."
2. "It's so hot in here!" — "Oh, ______ open the window."
3. "Why did you buy so many eggs?" — "I ______ a cake for Lucy's birthday."
4. "I don't think that new studio ______ very successful. The location is terrible."
5. "Oh no, I left my wallet at home!" — "Don't worry, I ______ for lunch."
6. "Any plans for the weekend?" — "Not really. I ______ at home and rest."
7. "The class ______ at 9 a.m. sharp, so don't be late tomorrow!"
8. "Watch out! You ______ fall off the reformer!"
1. 'm having (combinado)  |  2. I'll (decisão na hora)  |  3. 'm going to make (intenção — ela já tinha decidido antes de comprar)  |  4. will be (opinião com "I don't think")  |  5. 'll pay (oferta)  |  6. 'm just staying (plano; I'm just going to stay também serve)  |  7. starts (horário fixo)  |  8. 're going to (evidência — dá pra ver que vai cair)

Parte 3 — Sobre você

Complete as frases falando da sua vida de verdade.

This weekend I'm…

After my year off, I'm going to…

I think in ten years, Pilates will…

The next time I go to Brazil, I'm definitely going to…

One thing I promise I'll never do again is…

Respostas pessoais. Confira se: depois de I'm… veio verbo com -ing · depois de going to e will veio o verbo na forma base (going to travel, nunca "going to traveling") · won't = will not.

Exercício 2 — Writing

Parte 4 — My next chapter

Escreva uma mensagem longa (150 a 200 palavras) pra uma amiga no Brasil contando os seus planos pros próximos meses: o que já está combinado, o que você pretende fazer depois do ano sabático e o que você acha que vai acontecer.

Checklist: pelo menos 2 frases com present continuous (planos marcados), 3 com going to (intenções) e 3 com will (previsões, promessas ou ofertas).

Começo sugerido: "Hey girl! It's been forever! So, a lot is happening over here…"

Exemplo de resposta:
"Hey girl! It's been forever! So, a lot is happening over here. My parents are visiting in December — they're landing on the 15th and we're spending Christmas in Orlando. 🎄 After that, I'm going to start working again. I'm not going to go back to a full-time schedule, though. I'm going to teach Pilates three days a week and maybe take a course in clinical Pilates. Honestly, I think it'll be good for me. I'm sure I'll miss the free time, but I'll be more motivated. And next year I'm going to visit Brazil, I promise! I'll let you know the dates as soon as I buy the tickets. We'll go to that café we love!"

Pontos pra corrigir: plano com data/pessoa → present continuous · intenção → going to · promessa/opinião → will.

Parte 7 — Role-play: making plans

Notas:

Notas:

Notas:

Respostas pessoais. Pontos pra professora observar:
✔ a resposta usa a mesma estrutura da pergunta? (What are you doing…? → I'm…; Are you going to…? → Yes, I'm going to…)
✔ will aparece com probably / I think (ordem natural: I'll probably… / I probably won't…)
✔ pronúncia das contrações: I'll, you'll, it'll, won't, gonna
